## 3. A secure investment, guaranteed in writing

**Layout:** full-width text, or three short columns (Title · Pooled income · 4% floor).

**Heading:** A secure investment, guaranteed in writing.

**Body:**

You take part by buying one or more of the apartments in the building. The apartment is registered in your name, and you are free to sell it on the open market whenever you choose.

The income from all the apartments flows through our contract with you. As managers of the building, we decide who rents and when. You never need to worry about whether your apartment is standing empty — income is pooled and shared according to the value of each apartment.

Your safety net: a 4% return, guaranteed in our contract with you, regardless of what the market does. If we fall short, we cover the difference from our own pocket.

**Facts behind it:** title by *escritura* · 4% net of *valor público* is a covenant of the S.L. · developer pays the gap · no bank guarantee (do not use the word "insured").

**Sub-heading:** How your return is calculated

All apartments pay into one shared pool. From the building's income, your return is calculated according to your apartment's value, minus a monthly management fee based on how the apartment was actually rented that month:

- 0% — apartment stood empty
- 8% — long-term rental
- 10% — mid-term rental
- 15% — short-term rental

**Facts behind it:** *Valor Publicado* by unit · furniture by footprint · fee per the rental that month actually ran.
