02/

Protection continues through control

The rental strategy is not exposed to an outside owners’ vote.

El Fortín operates all ten apartments as one building: pricing, mix, cleaning, maintenance, and guest readiness stay in one team. There is no outside community of owners to restrict the rental strategy.

Whole-building control is a structural fact of ownership. It is not a claim of regulatory immunity, and it is not a tourist-use certificate on file.

07/

What is not guaranteed?

A serious investment includes the uncomfortable questions.

Construction payments

No aval bancario on buyer advances. Milestones organise when capital is called, not how it is ring-fenced.

Operating assumptions

~7% is a workbook mix, not a track record. Occupancy, mix, and rents can miss the model.

Pending documents

Tourist-use paperwork is claimed verbally. The file is not in this repository. Reservation templates are not on file.

Tax

This page is not tax advice. ITP versus IVA, IRNR, and home-country treatment stay with counsel.

Personal use

Up to 14 nights, free space, not peak season. Contractual terms are still unwritten. Secondary to the investment.

Covenant risk

The floor is an obligation of the developer’s company. It is not insured and not bank-backed.
