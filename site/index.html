Frequently asked questions.

How is the property managed?

The building is operated as a single, cohesive asset with a 50/50 initial mix of short-stay and corporate flex-living. We handle all bookings, cleaning, and maintenance in-house.

When do payouts begin?

Payouts begin immediately when operations start (turnkey delivery is scheduled for August 2027). There is no “ramp-up” void period for investors.

What are the management fees?

Fees scale by strategy: 8% for long-term, 10% for mid-term / corporate, and 15% for tourist rentals. The 4% net floor is calculated after these fees.

Are my funds protected during construction?

Payments are tied directly to certified construction milestones (40% and 80% completion), ensuring your capital is deployed only as physical progress is made on site.
