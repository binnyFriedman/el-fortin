How it works

The whole deal, in three parts.

1

You buy an apartment

A normal apartment, in your name. Furniture and taxes are in the price.

2

It rents as one building

We run all ten apartments for short and medium stays. Costs come out, profit is split by your share, paid monthly.

3

You sell whenever you want

A normal sale, on the open market — any rise in value is yours. The next owner can join the pool, or hold it as an ordinary apartment.

Your share of the capital = your share of the profit.

FAQ

The practical details.

How are payments staged?

Four equal payments of 25%: at reservation; when construction reaches 40%; when it reaches 80%, with furniture; then at the notary on handover.

Does the building have tourist-rental approval?

Yes. The licence covers the whole building.

What about my personal taxes?

Payouts arrive net of operating costs. Your own taxes depend on your situation — we walk through it with you before you buy.

How do owners stay informed?

Quarterly reports, with an investor app for live property data and votes on shared decisions.
