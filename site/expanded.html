Riba-roja de Túria, Valencia

In the historic centre, where demand is strongest.

Riba-roja de Túria is a high-demand rental market: the old town, Metro Line 9 to central Valencia, and twelve minutes from the airport. The building stands here, in the heart of it.

We run a rental programme, not ten separate lets.

The mix moves between long leases, mid-term stays, and short stays as demand shifts. We set it. Riba-roja is a busy logistics hub, and its industrial parks keep mid-term demand steady.

You take title. Rent is pooled.

You buy one or more of the apartments. Each one is registered in your name, and you are free to sell it on the open market whenever you choose.

Rent from all ten apartments is pooled and paid out in proportion to the value of each one. A vacant month in one apartment does not appear on your statement.

If the yearly return falls short of 4%, the company pays the difference.
