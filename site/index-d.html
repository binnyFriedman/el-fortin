We can do better than that.

Metro Line 9 connects the building to central Valencia. The airport is 12 minutes away. Riba-roja is a busy logistics hub, and its industrial parks generate steady demand for mid-term stays. Short-stay research shows 65% occupancy.

By moving between these markets as demand shifts, we expect close to 7% net in the first year of operation. After a few years of strong guest reviews, that climbs to 8% — and can go above 10%.

A secure investment, guaranteed in writing.

You take part by buying one or more of the apartments in the building. The apartment is registered in your name, and you are free to sell it on the open market whenever you choose.

The income from all the apartments flows through our contract with you. As managers of the building, we decide who rents and when. You never need to worry about whether your apartment is standing empty — income is pooled and shared according to the value of each apartment.

Your safety net: a 4% return, guaranteed in our contract with you, regardless of what the market does. If we fall short, we cover the difference from our own pocket.
