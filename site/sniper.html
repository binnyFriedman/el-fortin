How Your Income is Generated

Direct Ownership & Protected Income

You retain 100% direct, deeded ownership (Escritura) of the physical asset. You own the title, not a share in an opaque fund. Rental income is pooled across the building—meaning isolated unit vacancies cannot zero out your personal return. If pooled net falls below the contractual 4.0% floor of Valor Público, the developer covers the gap out of pocket.

Flexible Personal Vacations

Up to 14 nights a year for personal use, subject to availability and outside peak season. Exact booking terms are set in the owner agreement. The rest of the year, the unit generates passive, pooled income for you.

Technical & Legal FAQ

What is the capital call structure and construction schedule?

25% reservation · 25% at 40% of works · 25% at 80% (furniture included) · 25% at escritura. Turnkey delivery August 2027.

How should I think about Spanish tax on this investment?

You take direct deeded title to a specific apartment. Your own tax position (including any Spanish non-resident rules that may apply) depends on your residency and structure — we do not publish a tax outcome on this page. Bring your advisor; the data room covers the legal framework for them to review.

What is my required personal involvement?

Zero day-to-day. The management fee covers hands-off operations. You review financial reporting as issued; personal stays are limited to up to 14 nights a year (availability, not peak season — exact terms in the owner agreement).
