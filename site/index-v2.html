Contractual minimum

How income is shared

Income and costs run across all ten apartments as one operation, then paid in proportion to each owner’s capital. Your rate does not depend on which unit you hold. A weak month in one is carried by the other nine. A projection — not a fixed promise.

What the financial model must show

The document pack provided before reservation lists gross rent and occupancy by rental channel, management fees (8–15%, depending on rental structure), cleaning, utilities, maintenance, taxes, vacancy and downside scenarios. The >7% figure is meaningful only with those assumptions beside it.

Projections, not guarantees. The 4% minimum is a company covenant, not bank-backed. There is no aval bancario on buyer payments before completion.
