Follow the line

01/

Your side of the line

Direct ownership, without remote-landlord work.

You take title to a named apartment you will not live in full-time. Distance, furnishing, bookings, cleaning, and maintenance are the operator’s work—not a second job you run from the Netherlands, Belgium, or Germany.

You own

  • The deed A specific apartment, by name.
  • The income From the building’s rental strategy.
  • The option Up to 14 nights, free space, not peak season—secondary, and still being written into the contract.

You do not manage

  • Time and distance No remote landlord hours.
  • The building Bookings, cleaning, maintenance, guest readiness.
  • The mix Short-stay and corporate strategy stay with one team.

02/

The operator’s side

One team remains responsible for the entire building.

Management is not split across unrelated agencies. Pricing, rental mix, cleaning, and maintenance sit with the same operator who developed the building.
