# Engagement Terms — Binny ↔ Uriel

**Signed 2026-07-20.** Operative terms only, extracted from the signed marketing engagement agreement (boilerplate omitted). This is the commercial ground truth for the funnel: scope, budget, timeline, and economics all trace here.

Project: **Flex Living Riba-Roja (Valencia)** · Client: **Uriel Nabel / EL FORTIN CONSTRUCCIONES Y PATRIMONIO SL** · Provider: **Binny Friedman — marketing strategy & consulting.**

Working model: **results- and data-driven partnership.** Lean & Agile — marketing assets are "living" and change on field data. New/changed digital assets are decided *jointly*, and only when the feedback loop shows it's needed to increase the **quantity and quality of booked meetings** (the north-star metric).

---

## 1. Scope — Phase A (setup + month 1)

**The binding commitment is the outcome, not the deliverable list: generate qualified inbound leads *from the internet* and convert them into booked meetings.** The "how" is open-ended and at Binny's discretion. The itemized deliverables below are Binny's **initial articulation at signing** — a non-binding sketch of the likely build, kept for reference. Assets get chosen, added, or dropped to serve the outcome (per the jointly-decided, feedback-loop rule above), not because they appear here.

**Channel direction (Binny's call):** primary lever = **paid search**; second, exploratory track = **visibility in AI-engine / LLM answers (GEO/AEO — "SEO for the AI era")**, treated as R&D since the playbook is still immature.

1. **Strategic discovery** — deep-dive research call with the developer to mine past investor interactions (what worked, what the objections were); distill the trust narrative and define phase-1 target segments. *(Done — the 2026-07-20 call; see field log F7–F16 + `psychology-source-of-truth.md` §3.)*
2. **The funnel** — a fast digital path from ad-view to booked meeting:
   - **Conversion landing page**: project + financials + a **short screening questionnaire** + **direct calendar-booking integration into the developer's calendar**.
   - **Dynamic sales-support One-Pager**: existing raw materials adapted into an accessible digital doc that backs the developer's sales call, with **read/engagement tracking** per lead.
3. **Fixed control infrastructure** (flexible messaging, fixed measurement — runs across all 3 months):
   - **Analytics**: on-page + on-doc behavior (dwell time, where they linger) for immediate feedback.
   - **Minimal CRM**: status board for investors who booked meetings.
   - **Contact automation**: booking confirmations + reminders (email/WhatsApp) to the investor, plus alert to the developer.
4. **Paid campaign**: launch the first ad set on the chosen platform within the timeline below.

- **Live (active funnel + ads) within 30 working days** of signing *and* receiving raw materials.

## 4. Commercial terms

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Setup — Phase A (strategy, funnel build, optimization, month-1 management) | **€5,000** | one-time |
| Monthly retainer (months 2 & 3) | **€1,700 / mo** | daily campaign mgmt, engagement analysis, live message/asset updates |
| Success bonus | **€2,500 per unit sold** | per unit sold to an investor who came through the digital funnel; **no time limit, no dependency on the contract being active at the actual sale date** |

**Bonus offset:** each bonus first repays all funnel operating expenses (except platform media) — i.e. the €5,000 setup + €1,700/mo retainers. **Net bonus is paid only once cumulative bonuses exceed cumulative offset expenses.** (Performance-weighted: the first units' bonuses recoup fixed fees before any net bonus flows.)

**Lead-source definition:** an investor arriving via the campaign or digital funnel counts as a digital-marketing lead. Small project (≤9 units) → **source is established in direct conversation with the investor; no complex attribution mechanism.**

## 5. Payment & commitment

- **Paid in advance** at the start of each relevant activity month (setup for month 1; retainer for months 2 & 3).
- **Minimum commitment: 3 months, irrevocable / non-cancellable.**
- **Early sellout clause:** even if all units sell before month 3 ends, the full 3-month retainer is still owed.
- **After the minimum:** from month 4 on, either party may terminate on **30 days' written notice**.

## 6. Implications for the strategy docs

- **North-star metric is booked meetings (quantity × quality)** — the funnel's success metric (`01-direct-investor-funnel/` §5) resolves to this; every asset change must be justified by moving it.
- **Media budget is now firm: €1,500 initial-run ceiling** (supersedes the earlier ~€450/mo placeholder). Still a trickle-scale, high-intent spend — consistent with the 9-buyer depth-over-reach doctrine.
- **Binny's per-unit success fee (€2,500) is well under half the broker channel's €4,600–7,000 (F4)** — the web funnel is the cheaper acquisition path per buyer even before counting that its fixed fees are offset against bonuses.
- **Deliverables are not fixed scope.** The screening questionnaire, direct calendar booking, One-Pager, CRM, and booking automation are *options on the table*, not obligations — build them only if the feedback loop says they lift booked meetings. This also dissolves the earlier doctrine tension: nothing forces a screening/booking step ahead of the funnel's "name your fear" primary CTA (funnel §3/§4).
- **Channel focus is paid search + AI-engine visibility (GEO/AEO).** Paid search is the primary spend against the €1,500 cap; GEO/AEO is a time-funded R&D track. This narrows the funnel's channel menu (§3) — forums/YouTube stay optional, not committed.
- **SLA is 30 working days**; the funnel's internal ~11-day build plan sits comfortably inside that envelope.
