## Voice rules (one persona across every channel)

1. Candid peer, not promoter. Name the weakness before the prospect does (not city centre; no bank aval; town is unglamorous).
2. Floor first, target second, always labeled.
3. Specificity over superlatives: license numbers, comps, dates — not adjectives.
4. Every post CTA = one question the reader can answer ("what would stop you?"), never "exclusive access."
5. If a number isn't on this card, it doesn't get published — it gets sent to Binny to verify and add.
