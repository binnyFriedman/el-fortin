# Landing page v2 — EN concept

**Status:** draft. Run by Uriel before publishing.

---

## Writing rules

- Everything in the open. The reader verifies and concludes himself — his own conclusion is the one he keeps.
- Voice: thin "we". **"We" does the work, "I" carries the stake.** Uriel signs the hero and speaks to the end.
- Say each truth once, in its home frame. Give it air.
- If one fact contains another, say only the containing fact. Skip obvious conclusions.
- The floor is derived, never pushed — the reader computes it from public rents; the contract just writes it down.
- The upside is Uriel's bet, labeled as his bet, with the floor beneath it.
- Scarcity stated once, flat. Count apartments, never owners.
- Off the page, held for the meeting: money mechanics (4×25% beats, no aval), the whole-building tourist licence, the full research and contracts. Documents are read together, live; papers released after.
- The page converts to one thing: time with Uriel. Small step, warmly offered, easy to walk away from.

## Audiences — two campaigns, siblings not translations

| | EN — Spain-believer abroad | ES — local investor |
|---|---|---|
| Who | 50–60, Northern EU, €200k+ liquid, already sold on Spain | Knows Riba-roja and its rents |
| Beats | The flat he'd buy alone | The buy-to-let he'd run himself |
| Fear | Distance | 2008 |
| Meeting | Live video walk | Coffee at the site |
| Tone | Warm enough to hold the dream | Bare facts, zero adjectives |

Court the savings-parker and the local. Accept the yield-hunter without inflating for him. The price, the 2027 keys, and the calm tone filter out the rest on their own.

Page basics: type ≥17px, high contrast, calm static frames, printable. Under ~250 words total. Two possible actions, each under a minute.

## Color direction — Sunlit Mediterranean confidence

The page should feel like a serious business in a warm place: **trust first, Valencia second**. Not corporate finance navy, resort turquoise, or eco-property green. The sea appears as depth; the heat comes from sun-washed plaster and clay.

- **Mediterranean ink — `#153F4A`**: primary brand color; trust, competence, sea in shadow. Use for headings, navigation, primary buttons, and occasional dark frames.
- **Sun-washed plaster — `#F7F1E7`**: main ground; bright, human, architectural. Warmer and less clinical than white.
- **Valencia clay — `#C75F3C`**: emotional accent; heat, local roofs, hospitality. Use for small marks, rules, and selected details—not body copy or large sales panels.
- **Sea glass — `#6FA7A3`**: supporting field; calm, proximity to the Mediterranean, a forward-looking note without becoming “tech”.
- **Citrus light — `#E9B949`**: scarce highlight; sun and optimism. Reserve for proof points or a single small moment per frame.
- **Graphite — `#243238`**: long-form text and factual material; softer than black, still serious.

Keep the page roughly **70% plaster/white, 20% ink/graphite, 8% sea glass or clay, 2% citrus**. Primary actions are ink with white text; secondary actions are transparent with an ink border. Let photography provide most of the additional color, with warm natural light and no heavy teal-and-orange grade.

The core text pairings comfortably exceed WCAG AA: ink on plaster `10.15:1`, white on ink `11.40:1`, graphite on plaster `11.77:1`, graphite on sea glass `4.87:1`, and ink on citrus `6.24:1`. Clay is an accent, not a text background.

### 02 — Purpose *(image: unpolished proof the site exists today)*

> ## Built for one purpose.
>
> I bought this place — the metro to Valencia runs from outside the building.
> My team and I designed every apartment to be the best revenue engine we could make.
> Construction is underway. Keys in 2027.
> Our previous building — delivered August 2025.

### 03 — The comparison *(EN-only frame; ES gets its own here)*

> ## If you were going to buy in Spain anyway.
>
> **Buying alone:** tenants found from abroad · an agency at arm's length · resale surprises · long-term rent, ~4% on a good day.
>
> **Buying here:** a new build · deed in your name · we run the letting · that same ~4% is the *floor*.

### 04 — The math

> ## The whole calculation.
>
> Rent collected − running costs − our fee = **your share, monthly.**
>
> Each apartment earns the way it earns best — long stays, corporate mid-stays, holiday nights.
> Our fee is a percentage of the income.
> Income from all ten apartments runs as one operation, paid out by investment size.
>
> Local rents, checkable: [comp + source]

### 05 — Floor and upside

> ## The worst case is an apartment, rented.
>
> If short stays underperform, yours rents long-term.
> Riba-roja rents are public — check what that pays. [comp + source]
> The contract writes that down, in ink.
>
> ## The upside is my bet.
>
> Short stays here are villas with pools — apartments like these don't exist yet.
> The demand does; I watch it arrive every week.
> Reviews lift ranking; ranking lifts the rate.
> Around us, rents rose 11–14.5% a year through 2024–25.

### 06 — The relationship *(image: the man himself, someone you'd have coffee with)*

> ## I'm Uriel.
>
> Father, husband, builder — 20 years in the industry. I live here.
> My savings are in this building. My name is on it.
>
> If you buy here, we'll be working together for years.
> So take your time with me. Ask everything.
>
> When something needs doing at 2 a.m., my phone rings. Yours stays quiet.

### 07 — The count *(one line, maximum air)*

> Ten apartments — one sold, nine available.
> The first buyer came in person, and bought out of love for Valencia.

### 08 — The choice *(image: two configurations to study and pick from)*

> ## Two apartment types.
>
> **Type A** — [beds · m² · from €X all-in · your monthly share at the floor: €—]
> **Type B** — [beds · m² · from €Y all-in · your monthly share at the floor: €—]
>
> Same building, same management, same math. The difference is the size of your stake.

### 09 — Walk the land *(the close)*

> ## Walk the land with me.
>
> I'll take you around the site — live, on video. Twenty minutes, from your home.
> The documents and research come with me: we'll read them together.
>
> Visiting Valencia? Come in person. The coffee's on me.
>
> **Pick a time** · or **write first** — whichever you prefer.
> See you soon.
