## How to read a frame

Hide the words. The graphic must still say the desire. If it does not, it is the wrong graphic.

| Job | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Identify** | What building, what stake, what person. |
| **Prove** | What exists, has progressed, or has been granted. |
| **Explain** | How the building or the money works. |
| **Locate** | What this ground in Riba-roja feels like. |
| **Desire** | What a guest or an owner will value here. |

One job per frame. Captions sit outside the image. Do not write on faces, doors, plans, or the object being proved.

**Desktop** is the first viewport at ~1280–1440. Copy and image share the screen.  
**Mobile** is the authoritative order. Type answers the five-second test before any drawing.

## Crops

| Kind | Ratio | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building render (hero) | Native | The product fills the frame. No overlay, no crop that loses the façade. |
| Cutaway illustration | Native, with margin | White / Sunlit Plaster. Never 16:9-cropped into a banner. Appears once. |
| Construction | Desktop wide · Mobile full width | Documentary. Do not grade it. |
| Uriel | 4:5 | Environment stays. |
| Interiors | 1:1 or 4:5 | Keep the room. Do not force 16:9. |
| Plans | Whatever keeps the rooms readable | Type A as it stands is too panoramic for a phone. |

One corner radius, or none. A 1px Deep Sea line at low weight if a drawing needs an edge. No shadows. No glass cards. No coloured frames.
