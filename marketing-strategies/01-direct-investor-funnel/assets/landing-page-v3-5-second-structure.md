# Landing page v3 — five-second structure

**Status:** structural draft. Working English copy: `landing-page-v3-copy.md`. Copy still requires Uriel review and normal claim approval.

## The five-second test

Without scrolling, a visitor must understand:

1. **What is it?** One purpose-built managed building of ten apartments near Valencia.
2. **How do I own part of it?** Through title in the building — an apartment deed. A buyer is not capped at one deed.
3. **What does it cost?** From €236,141 all-in.
4. **Who does the work?** El fortin team led by Uriel Nabel.

If any of these answers requires interpretation, the first screen has failed.

The page is not trying to persuade everyone. It lets the right visitor recognize himself quickly:

> I want to own property in Spain. I can invest approximately €236k. I do not want to become a remote landlord. I am comfortable buying before the 2027 handover.

## Page rule

**Recognition first. Evidence second. Relationship third.**

Do not open with lifestyle, a founder biography, investment terminology, or a high-yield claim. Those can only help after the visitor knows what is being offered.

## 02 — The model: show the mechanism

**Purpose:** answer “How does this actually work?” in one glance.

### Heading

> ## One building. One operation. Ten apartment deeds.

### Four-step diagram

1. **You buy into the building**
  Your ownership is recorded through the deed to one apartment.
2. **We finish and furnish the whole operation**
  All ten apartments are delivered together in 2027.
3. **We run the building**
  Long, medium and tourist stays are managed as one portfolio.
4. **You receive your share**
  Building income − running costs − management fee, distributed by ownership share.

One sentence below:

> The building is the product. The apartment deed defines your ownership inside it.

Avoid “vertically integrated,” “asset management,” “investment vehicle,” and similar industry language.

## 03 — Proof that this is real

**Purpose:** replace promotional confidence with visible evidence before asking the visitor to compare or believe the calculation.

### Heading

> ## Not a proposed fund. A building already underway.

Use dated construction media, the cropped façade and a compact evidence strip:

- construction underway
- building licence number
- tourist rental granted
- funded with Uriel's own capital
- architect and constructor
- handover in 2027
- previous El Fortín project, delivered August 2025

Offer:

> **See the construction record**

This opens a light proof layer, not the full data room.

Pre-handover payment protection belongs here as soon as the required documentation is available. Until then, do not imply that staged payments are protected.

## 04 — The alternative: why not buy alone?

**Purpose:** make the buyer's real choice visible after El Fortín has established credibility.

### Heading

> ## Property in Spain without a second job in Spain.

### Side-by-side comparison

**Buy an apartment alone**

- Find, prepare and furnish the property remotely
- Appoint and coordinate separate agents
- Depend on one apartment and one booking calendar

**Buy at El Fortín**

- Buy into one purpose-built, tourist-rental-approved building
- Work with one local team from construction through letting
- Participate in the result of one ten-apartment operation

Do not make the comparison about yield alone. The meaningful difference is ownership with less work and less operational distance.

## 05 — The conservative case

**Purpose:** establish credibility before presenting upside.

### Heading

> ## What ordinary rent leaves after every cost.

### Core message

> If flexible stays disappoint, the operator moves capacity toward the long-term rental market.

Present the calculation with gross income on the left and what reaches the owner on the right:

- purchase price and all-in capital
- evidenced monthly rent
- vacancy allowance
- running costs
- management fee
- net monthly amount
- net annual percentage

Then state:

> **Realistic base: approximately 4% net.**

The calculation must be understandable without opening a spreadsheet. Do not publish it until the reconciled unit-level financial model exists.

## 06 — The rental advantage

**Purpose:** show why this is not an ordinary buy-to-let apartment after the conservative case is understood.

### Heading

> ## Built and approved to earn in more than one way.

Explain briefly:

- the whole building was designed for managed rental from the beginning;
- tourist rental has been granted;
- the operator can rotate units between long, medium and tourist stays;
- the operation can pursue the strongest real demand instead of locking each apartment into one rental model;
- the blended target is approximately 7% net;
- it is a target, not a promise.

Use the pool terrace or a strong interior here. At this point the visitor understands the asset, so the image can introduce enjoyment without turning the page into resort advertising.

## 07 — The accountable person

**Purpose:** turn a credible project into a relationship.

### Heading

> ## His money built it. His team stays to run it.

### Draft content

> I'm Uriel Nabel. I am El Fortín's only development investor. My own capital built this project, and I will remain responsible for its operation after handover.
>
> I am not selling a project financed by distant investors and walking away. If you buy here, my money and name remain beside yours. We will work together for years—so ask me the uncomfortable questions before you decide.

Support with:

- one natural portrait;
- concise, verified career evidence;
- the fact that Uriel is the sole development investor;
- the previously delivered project.

Keep this section human and short. A biography is not a substitute for documents.

---

## 08 — Choose your ownership inside the building

**Purpose:** make entry into the managed building concrete enough to act on.

### Heading

> ## One operation. Two apartment sizes. Nine still available.

For each type show:

- floor plan
- useful and built area
- all-in price
- estimated long-term rent
- conservative net monthly amount
- floor and orientation
- current availability

Each deed represents a specific apartment, but neither apartment type is presented as a standalone rental product. Both enter the same managed building, cost structure and income operation.

Do not hide all prices behind a form. A visitor who cannot establish affordability will not become a better lead by submitting contact details.

## 09 — The meeting

**Purpose:** offer a small, private next step after the visitor understands the deal.

### Heading

> ## See the site and question the numbers.

### Draft close

> Uriel will walk through the building live on video, then open the calculation and documents with you. Twenty minutes. No obligation to continue.

Primary action:

> **Choose a time**

Secondary action:

> **Write to Uriel first**

No generic “Request more information” form.

## Reading speeds

The page must work at three depths:

### Five seconds

- one managed ten-apartment building near Valencia
- ownership held through an apartment deed
- the whole building is managed for me
- purpose-built and approved for tourist rental
- €236,141 all-in
- 2027 handover

### Thirty seconds

- one managed building whose ownership is divided into ten apartment deeds
- realistic base around 4% net
- higher blended target is separate
- construction is underway
- founder-funded by the person who will operate it
- nine apartments remain

### Three minutes

- complete calculation
- operating mechanism
- proof of construction and previous delivery
- person responsible
- ownership choice inside the building
- next-step meeting

Every section begins with the conclusion. Supporting detail may follow, but the visitor should never have to read a paragraph to discover the point.

## Interface constraints

- No conventional navigation on the campaign page.
- One idea per section.
- Headings must make sense when read alone.
- Body paragraphs: maximum three lines on desktop.
- Minimum body size: 17px.
- Use real project images before lifestyle imagery.
- Keep the main price visible more than once, but scarcity only once.
- No carousel; it hides information and asks the visitor to wait.
- No automatic video or animated financial counters.
- Mobile order is the authoritative order.
- Primary action changes with readiness: **See how it works** near the top, **Choose a time** only after the evidence.

## Casa Ivy as visual guidance—not positioning

`https://casaivygroup.com/investors`

Casa Ivy is the closest visual reference found so far because it combines:

- Mediterranean desire with investment seriousness;
- editorial typography rather than property-portal styling;
- cinematic architecture photography;
- strong light and dark section rhythm;
- numbered explanations;
- financial evidence presented as designed content;
- generous whitespace and disciplined accents.

Its market position is different:

> **Casa Ivy:** “We are experienced operators who can find and improve the right investment for you.”
>
> **El Fortín:** “I built this managed ten-apartment building with my own money. You enter through an apartment deed; my team operates the building as one.”

Casa Ivy sells a platform, a pipeline and operating capability. El Fortín sells one understandable asset and direct accountability.

### Borrow

- the editorial page rhythm;
- one strong idea per screen;
- serif display headlines with restrained sans-serif details;
- the numbered process band immediately below the hero;
- alternating immersive and quiet sections;
- designed financial proof rather than spreadsheet styling;
- large architectural images with minimal interface around them;
- the feeling that every detail has been intentionally selected.

### Translate

- Casa Ivy charcoal → **El Fortín Deep Sea**
- Casa Ivy orange → **El Fortín Sun Gold**
- Casa Ivy cream → **El Fortín Sunlit Plaster**
- dark Costa del Sol sunset → bright Valencia sky, limestone and warm daylight
- “strategy call” → “see how it works” first, then a direct walk with Uriel
- operator case study → building, licence, founder capital and conservative calculation

### Do not borrow

- “build wealth” language;
- an immediate consultation sell;
- multiple investment pathways;
- advisor or deal-sourcing positioning;
- aggressive performance case studies;
- a dark luxury-resort atmosphere;
- generic claims about knowing the market better than the visitor.

## Section-by-section evaluation

### 01 — First screen

**Verdict: keep the content logic; change the visual treatment.**

What remains right:

- the offer is identifiable within five seconds;
- ownership, management, price, handover and availability are visible;
- the first action asks the visitor to understand, not immediately surrender contact details.

What changes after the Casa Ivy review:

- use a large architectural image with confident editorial typography;
- keep the image brighter than Casa Ivy: open sky, sun and real building materials;
- use Deep Sea for the navigation/text anchor and Sun Gold for one primary action;
- retain “One building. Ten apartments. Managed as one.” because it names the actual product;
- place the price, handover and availability in one clean horizontal fact line;
- remove any secondary action that competes with “See how it works” on mobile.

Do not use the pool as the hero. It would make El Fortín look like a resort before the visitor understands the asset.

### 02 — The model

**Verdict: keep and make more visual.**

This is El Fortín's equivalent of Casa Ivy's numbered capability band, but ours explains one building product and the apartment-deed ownership mechanism rather than four services.

Use:

- a compact `01–04` strip directly after the hero;
- one verb per step: **Enter · Complete · Operate · Receive**;
- the axonometric drawing immediately below for visitors who continue;
- short labels first, details on demand.

Do not let this become an “asset-management platform” section. Its job is simply to remove structural confusion.

### 03 — The alternative

**Verdict: keep, compress and move after project proof.**

The comparison is strategically important, but it currently arrives before the visitor has enough reason to trust El Fortín.

Change:

- first prove that the building, permission and founder commitment are real;
- then compare “buy alone” with “buy here”;
- reduce each side to three concrete differences;
- use open typography rather than two heavy cards.

The section should sell less work and less distance—not claim that every independent apartment purchase is inferior.

### 04 — The conservative case

**Verdict: keep; make it the central proof moment.**

Casa Ivy uses a dramatic performance case study. El Fortín should not imitate the claim, but should imitate the clarity and visual confidence.

Our equivalent is:

> **What ordinary rent leaves after every cost.**

Show:

- starting capital;
- monthly rent;
- each cost;
- the management fee;
- the resulting monthly amount;
- the approximate 4% net base.

Use a before/after-style calculation layout: gross on the left, what reaches the owner on the right. No calculator interaction is needed for the first version.

### 05 — The rental advantage

**Verdict: keep, but lead with permission and design—not aspiration.**

This is where El Fortín separates from a normal buy-to-let:

- the whole building was designed for managed rental;
- tourist rental is granted;
- all ten apartments can be operated as one system;
- the model can move between long, medium and tourist demand.

Use the pool or a strong interior here. By this point the visitor knows it is an asset, so the image can introduce enjoyment without turning the whole page into resort advertising.

The ~7% blended target belongs here, visually separated from the conservative case.

### 06 — Proof that this is real

**Verdict: strengthen and move directly after the model.**

Casa Ivy borrows authority from operating history. El Fortín's authority comes from specificity and personal exposure.

Lead with:

- construction underway;
- tourist rental granted;
- Uriel's own capital funded the development;
- previous El Fortín project delivered;
- exact building licence;
- handover in 2027.

Use one strong headline:

> **Not a proposed fund. A building already underway.**

The section should combine dated construction media, the cropped façade and a compact fact strip. It must feel documentary, not defensive.

### 07 — The accountable person

**Verdict: keep; make founder capital the opening fact.**

Casa Ivy presents a capable team. El Fortín has a more powerful and more personal truth:

> **His money built it. His team stays to run it.**

The section should include:

- one natural portrait;
- Uriel as sole development investor;
- his continued operational responsibility;
- one short verified competence line;
- an invitation to ask uncomfortable questions.

Avoid a long résumé. The visitor needs to understand alignment and accountability before biography.

### 08 — Choose your ownership inside the building

**Verdict: keep; present two ways to enter the same product, not a collection of separate products.**

Casa Ivy can ask prospects to discuss a thesis because it has a pipeline. El Fortín must show the actual thing being sold.

Use:

- two deeded apartment sizes within the same managed building;
- one interior render and one floor plan for each;
- price and size visible without interaction;
- conservative monthly result;
- exact availability.

Do not create filters, search controls or portal-style cards. The buyer is not browsing unrelated apartments. He is choosing the size of his ownership inside one operation.

### 09 — The meeting

**Verdict: keep the low-pressure close; adopt Casa Ivy's visual confidence, not its sales framing.**

Casa Ivy asks for a strategy call because it needs to diagnose and source. El Fortín can offer something more concrete:

> **Walk through this building with the person building it.**

Use:

- one primary action: **Choose a time**;
- one quieter alternative: **Write to Uriel first**;
- a clear description of the twenty-minute walk;
- no long qualification form.

The CTA should feel like access to the principal, not entry into a sales funnel.

## Revised page order after evaluation

1. **Offer** — understand it in five seconds.
2. **Mechanism** — Enter · Complete · Operate · Receive.
3. **Proof** — real building, granted use, founder capital, previous delivery.
4. **Comparison** — buying alone versus buying inside this operation.
5. **Conservative calculation** — ordinary-rent base after costs.
6. **Rental advantage** — purpose-built flexibility and target upside.
7. **Uriel** — the accountable person who remains beside the owner.
8. **Ownership options** — two deeded apartment sizes, one managed operation.
9. **Meeting** — walk through the site, numbers and documents.

This follows Casa Ivy's useful rhythm—offer, process, proof, depth, contact—while preserving El Fortín's single-project and developer-direct position.

## Color direction — Valencia outside, the building inside

The first palette overfit the interiors. It was tasteful, but it could have belonged to apartments almost anywhere.

El Fortín must first locate the visitor in Valencia:

- brilliant blue sky;
- deep Mediterranean water;
- strong golden sunlight;
- warm white architecture;
- sand and limestone;
- small touches of terracotta and citrus.

The interiors then make that regional promise tangible through plaster, pale oak, woven cane, clay, sage and walnut.

The emotional combination is **blue + sun gold**, grounded by warm plaster and Deep Sea:

> **Sunny Spain outside. Serious ownership underneath.**

Avoid royal navy with metallic gold—it would read as private banking. Avoid tropical turquoise—it would read as a resort. The blue should feel like open Valencia sky and Mediterranean depth; the yellow should feel like sunlight, not luxury trim.

### What relevant competitors do

**Casa Ivy — charcoal, ivory and saffron**

- **Meaning:** wealth, sunset, luxury hospitality and active opportunity.
- **Roles:** charcoal establishes financial seriousness; saffron drives calls to action and highlights performance; cinematic photography supplies desire.
- **Lesson:** the dark/light contrast is effective, but the orange emphasis feels more promotional than El Fortín should.

**Kategora / Kora — black, white and natural photography**

- **Meaning:** scale, architecture and institutional confidence.
- **Roles:** the interface almost disappears; the project renders carry all location and lifestyle color.
- **Lesson:** restraint makes an investment offer easier to understand. El Fortín should borrow the discipline, not the coldness.

**Lumina Saltus — forest green and muted gold**

- **Meaning:** nature, retreat, exclusivity and founder-led intimacy.
- **Roles:** green creates an immersive world; gold marks the premium promise.
- **Lesson:** strong when the landscape is the product. Too dark and thematic for a practical apartment investment.

**Veseer — warm stone and graphite**

- **Meaning:** boutique judgment, discretion and design authority.
- **Roles:** whitespace communicates selectivity; graphite does nearly all functional work; images arrive as considered objects.
- **Lesson:** this is the closest tonal reference for El Fortín's direct, principal-led relationship.

### Recommended El Fortín palette

**1. Mediterranean Blue — `#176B87`**

- Primary brand color.
- Navigation, section labels, links and selected structural fields.
- Carries place and trust without becoming finance navy.

**2. Open Sky — `#72BDE0`**

- Spacious supporting fields, diagrams and occasional full-width breathing sections.
- Never use behind long body copy.
- Connects directly to the exterior renders.

**3. Sun Gold — `#F2BC3B`**

- Primary calls to action, selected facts and one decisive highlight per screen.
- The visual meeting point between blue sky and Valencia sun.
- Use as flat sunlight, never as metallic gold.

**4. Sunlit Plaster — `#FAF5E8`**

- Main page background.
- Matches the limestone, walls and pale flooring in the renders.
- Keeps the bright palette warm rather than clinical.

**5. Warm Sand — `#DCCBAA`**

- Secondary surfaces, calculation panels and quiet separators.
- Bridges the blue/gold brand layer with the warm interiors.

**6. Valencia Clay — `#C96843`**

- Small human and architectural details.
- Drawn from curtains, bedding, dining chairs, brick and roof tile.
- Never compete with Sun Gold for the primary action.

**7. Deep Sea — `#173D4A`**

- Body copy, headings, primary dark sections and serious evidence.
- White text when used as a field.
- Grounds the optimism of sky and sun.

### Usage balance

- **55%** Sunlit Plaster
- **20%** Deep Sea
- **12%** Mediterranean Blue
- **6%** Warm Sand
- **4%** Open Sky
- **2%** Sun Gold
- **1%** Valencia Clay

Color must clarify hierarchy:

- **Deep Sea:** read this and trust this.
- **Mediterranean Blue:** this belongs to Valencia and El Fortín.
- **Sun Gold:** act here or notice this key fact.
- **Open Sky:** breathe and understand.
- **Warm Sand:** supporting information.
- **Valencia Clay:** human or architectural detail.

Do not assign a different accent color to every section.

## Image direction — frame, do not fight

### Lesson from the Casa Ivy owners page

Casa Ivy's manager section contains the right strategic idea and the wrong photograph.

The words say:

- experienced founder;
- personally accountable;
- locally present;
- professional operator;
- the person who remains available when something matters.

The portrait says:

- quick phone selfie;
- harsh overhead light;
- very close lens;
- generic indoor background;
- artificial circular crop;
- no evidence of Málaga, property or operational work.

The image wins. A visitor processes the face, light, posture and environment before reading the biography. Strong words cannot repair a frame that communicates low effort or the wrong role.

Casa Ivy also repeats a decorative keys image through the page. Repetition creates visual continuity, but it consumes valuable frames without adding evidence. El Fortín should not use photography as wallpaper.

### El Fortín image rule

Every image must perform one job before words are added:

1. **Identify** — what building or person is this?
2. **Prove** — what exists, has progressed or has been granted?
3. **Explain** — how is the building or operation structured?
4. **Locate** — what does Valencia or Riba-roja genuinely feel like?
5. **Desire** — what will guests and owners value here?

If an image does none of these with the text hidden, remove it.

Words may sharpen the meaning of an image. They must not manufacture meaning that the photograph failed to capture.

### What the current renders contain

**Interiors**

- The strongest and most coherent set.
- Dominated by warm neutrals, terracotta, sage, oak and walnut.
- They feel residential, calm and intentionally designed—not like generic white investment units.
- Use their natural color; do not add teal-and-orange grading.

**Pool terrace**

- The brightest image: blue sky and water, green planting, fuchsia flowers and warm stone.
- It can easily push the page toward resort marketing.
- Use after the ownership and rental model are clear, with a factual caption such as “shared roof terrace.”

**Courtyard with cars**

- Strong architecture, planting and material detail.
- The Porsche and Mini make the image read as a luxury-status advertisement.
- Crop toward the courtyard, stairs and screening. Do not make the cars a central page image.

**Front façade**

- Important because it identifies the actual building.

- Crop tighter around the façade and entrance; slightly reduce highlights and car saturation.
- Best used as project proof rather than as an aspirational lifestyle image.

**Axonometric drawing**

- Excellent for explaining the ten-apartment operation.
- Its white ground belongs naturally on Sunlit Plaster.
- Give it a thin Deep Sea line or generous whitespace—never a colored overlay.

### Draft readiness

**Build a real responsive wireframe now.**

The page structure, palette, visual reference and first-draft asset set are sufficiently defined. More abstract planning would now cost more than it saves.

The first implementation should establish:

- section rhythm and reading speed;
- mobile order;
- real image crops;
- type scale;
- Valencia palette roles;
- the conservative-calculation layout;
- CTA progression.

It should not spend time yet on:

- final animation;
- elaborate transitions;
- a calculator;
- a CMS;
- perfect final copy;
- speculative image generation.

The wireframe becomes the next planning tool. Decisions will be cheaper when made against the actual images at real browser widths.

### Framing system

- Exterior images may run wide.
- Interior images should use `1:1` or `4:5`.
- Technical drawings sit on Sunlit Plaster with generous margins.
- Use one consistent small corner radius, or none; do not alternate styles.
- Captions sit outside the image, never over important architectural detail.
- A `1px` Deep Sea border at low visual weight is enough when an image needs separation.
- Avoid shadows, floating glass cards and colored image frames.
- Compose new photography with intentional negative space when text will share the frame.
- Do not place text over faces, doors, plans, building lines or the object being proved.
- Never repeat an image merely to fill a section.
- Before approving a frame, hide the copy and ask what the image says on its own.

The palette should recede. The apartment is the color.
