## Fear 1 — "Rent will not come in"

Fair question — and you're right to ignore brochure rents.

What we can evidence today (live comparables, July 2026): new, furnished apartments near the metro in this corridor rent at ~€16/m² — a 47 m² unit at €850–950/month, a 57 m² at €1,050–1,150. At those rents, an all-long-term building nets around 4% on pre-sale capital — which is roughly where our contractual floor sits. In other words: the *worst realistic case* is approximately the floor, and anything from mid-term or seasonal letting stacks on top. *(deal doc §5b, §7)*

The demand side isn't the risk here: Valencia is Spain's fastest-renting major market (24% of listings gone in under 24h; 32–38 enquiries per listing). The honest open question is price level, not occupancy. *(deal doc §5c)*
