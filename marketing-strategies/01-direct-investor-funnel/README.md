## 0. Operational axioms (constraints, not choices)

| Axiom | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Binny operates from Israel; Uriel is on the ground in Valencia | Two-node trust network: Uriel = face + proof engine; Binny = strategist + dialogue operator |
| Uriel independently runs a local broker channel (F5: 2–3% commission, heavenhouse.es + others) | Clean division of labor: Uriel owns B2B on the ground, this funnel owns direct web. **Consistency rule (P12): identical claims and pricing across both channels** — a prospect who hears different numbers from a broker and the landing page is lost permanently |
| **Web-only funnel; Binny's personal contacts are off-limits; Binny has no deep Israeli-market knowledge** | Attention must be **intercepted from existing intent** (search, communities), not created by interruption ads or network access |
| Budget per signed engagement (`../engagement-terms.md`): €5k setup covers month 1; **media capped at €1,500 for the initial run, paid direct to platforms** | Low-volume high-intent channels; a €1,500 initial-run cap (~€10–20/day) is enough at 9-buyer scale |
| Direct end investors only — no finders/agents | We are entirely in the P1 funnel (gut feel about the person); the P2 defensibility track is out of scope |
| Ticket: all-in from **€236,141** (developer-verified canonical entry; deal doc §4) | Buyer = private individual with ~€200k+ liquid seeking EU brick; low volume, high trust threshold |
| Inventory: 9 of 10 units (deal doc §1) | We need **a handful of buyers, not an audience**. Depth beats reach everywhere |
| Relationship is new; Binny must show results | Instrument the funnel from day 1 (named fears, replies, calls booked) so progress is visible even before a sale |

The 9-buyers reality is the single most important design input: a mass funnel is the wrong shape. This is a **trust pipeline**, engineered around psychology, that needs ~9 conversions over the project's life.

## 1. Segment choice (derived, ranked — v2 per F1)

**The governing insight (F1 + prospect theory, P14):** a yield is judged against a *reference point*, never in absolute terms. Israelis shopping overseas deals carry an 8–10%+ anchor from Georgia/Dubai/Greece-style pitches — our honest 4%-floor/7%-target *loses that comparison before we speak*. Conservative EU investors carry a 2–3% anchor (deposits, domestic rental yields) — the same numbers sound excellent. Same figure, opposite verdicts, purely reference-point psychology. And the buyer whose anchor we fit is also the buyer whose *psychology fits the product*: loss-averse capital preservation (deal doc §2), not yield-chasing.

| Rank | Segment | Why (principles + data) | Access (web-only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Conservative EU capital-preservers** (Germans, Dutch, Nordics, Brits, French — English-first assets) | F1 (7% beats their anchor); P14 loss-averse product fit; P3 lower ambiguity — Spain is the *normalized* foreign-property dream of Northern Europe (B6 partly pre-flipped: "people like me buy Spanish flats" is already a cultural script); same legal zone | **Intent capture**: Google search on buy-in-Valencia/Spain queries; expat & investor forums; YouTube search |
| 2 | **EU-based Jewish/Israeli expats** (Berlin, Amsterdam, London, Madrid/Barcelona) | P10 unity retained *without* Binny's personal contacts; EU reference points (F1 applies less — they bank in euros) | Online communities, later referral from segment 1 conversations |
| 3 | **Argentinian/Latin community in Spain** | P6 congruence (lived the savings-loss story); P10 via Uriel's origin | Uriel's on-ground network — his channel, not the web funnel's |
| — | **Israelis seeking high-yield overseas deals** | **Deprioritized on F1**: reference-point mismatch means honest numbers lose; segment creates pressure to overclaim = the P12 failure mode. Revisit only with a capital-preservation-framed offer tested elsewhere first | — |

**Decision: launch on segment 1 in English, web-intent channels.** The yield-hunter segment isn't "harder" — it's wrong for an honest 4%-floor product.

## 2. The belief ladder (what must become true in the buyer's head)

A person wires €250k when all of these are true. Each funnel stage exists to flip exactly one or two:

| # | Belief | Principle | Primary instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | "This is real and verifiable" | P12 | License numbers, delivered Puzol building, data room |
| B2 | "The man behind it is competent" | P8 | Track record first: Puzol Aug 2025, ex-SAP/Siemens |
| B3 | "The man behind it is honest" | P7 (after B2) | Candor block: not Valencia centro; no bank aval today |
| B4 | "I understand what I'd own" | P3, P4 | Fluent anchors (Valencia · Metro L1 · deed in your name); one-screen explanation |
| B5 | "My downside is bounded" | P14 | Deed, sell-anytime, 4% floor stated with its real backing (deal doc §7) |
| B6 | "People like me do this" | P10 | Unity channel, group settings, early-buyer stories (only real ones) |
| B7 | "Waiting costs me something" | P16 | True count: 10 units, 1 gone — never manufactured urgency |

**Diagnostic rule:** every stalled lead is stalled on a *specific* belief. The funnel's feedback mechanism (§4, step 3) exists to find out which — that's also your reporting metric to Uriel.

## 3. Funnel shape

**Attention doctrine (the anti-overthinking rule):** we do not *create* attention with interruption ads — we **intercept intent that already exists**. Someone typing "buy apartment Valencia" or asking a forum "is a Spanish rental flat a good investment?" has already flipped B4 and half of B6 on their own. At 9-buyer scale, a trickle of high-intent visitors beats any volume of interrupted scrollers, and it's the only attention shape that fits the budget.

| Intent channel | Cost shape | Why it works (principle) |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid search (primary lever)** — Google ads on buy/invest Valencia & Spain-property queries, EN (+DE later) | €1,500 initial-run cap (`../engagement-terms.md`) | Intent already formed; ad copy only needs the fluent anchor + honesty marker (P4, P9) |
| **AI-engine visibility / GEO-AEO (exploratory R&D)** — being cited in LLM/AI-overview answers to the same buy/invest-Valencia questions | Time only | Same intent-capture logic one layer up: buyers increasingly ask an AI before they search. Playbook immature — treat as R&D, instrument what moves booked meetings |
| Forums & communities, YouTube/SEO content *(optional, not committed)* | Time only | Value-first answers / face + candor in personal voice (P5, P7, P9); slow but compounding — run only if the feedback loop justifies it |

```
[Attention]  Intercepted intent: search ad / forum answer / video   — flips B2, starts B4
     ↓
[Landing]    One page: anchor → competence → story → candor → number → count
             CTA: "What still worries you?" (choose or write)   — B3, B4, B5
     ↓
[Dialogue]   WhatsApp, one fear → one answer → one question back   — B3, B5
     ↓
[Live]       1:1 call with Uriel; documents reviewed together on screen                  — B1, B2
     ↓
[Evidence]   Relevant papers released after the meeting; no document dumps               — B1
     ↓
[Close]      Buyer's own lawyer + reservation   — B7 only here, with the real count
```

Design notes (each traced):

- **Video before landing page** — P5: the face is the trust channel; P1: the person is the product. Content order obeys the sequencing law (psych doc §4).
- **Primary CTA is the live document review; “name your fear” is the lower-commitment alternative** — decisive buyers book directly, while cautious buyers can start with one question. Always close with explicit freedom to refuse (P15).
- **WhatsApp, not email, for segment 1** — native medium, conversational, lets Binny run the 1:1 loop at low cost. One fear → one focused answer → one question back. No document dumps.
- **1:1 calls with Uriel are the default live step** — P1: the investor is deciding on the person, and a 1:1 call is the highest-bandwidth read of a person; it's also Uriel's proven strength and has the lowest scheduling friction at trickle-volume. Uriel takes the call from the site or the finished Puzol building when possible: on-ground proof (B1) + face (P5) in one shot.
- **Group Q&A is a phase-2 weapon, never the default** — its sole unique job is B6 + credible scarcity (P16): other serious buyers visible in the room is the one thing a 1:1 can't provide. But social proof is symmetric — an empty room broadcasts "nobody wants this." **Trigger condition: run it only when ≥5–8 qualified, fear-named leads can be invited.** Until then, 1:1 only.
- **Documents in the meeting; data room after it** — identity earns attention and paper closes, but a download before a live conversation turns the project into one more spreadsheet. Uriel reviews the relevant evidence on screen, then releases the exact papers the buyer or counsel needs.
- **Scarcity only at close** — P16: the 9-of-10 count is credible precisely because we never lead with it.

## 4. Asset list (build order, owner, spec)

Minimal set. Each asset flips specific beliefs and obeys the sequencing law (psych doc §4).

**Drafts live in [`assets/`](assets/):** `landing-page-copy.md` (A2) · `fear-answers.md` (A3) · `search-campaign.md` (A6). Placeholder convention: `[⏳ U-Q#]` keys to `uriel-call-questions.md`. **Build with placeholders, never publish with them.**

### A1 — Founder story video (60–90s, vertical, iPhone) — Uriel shoots, Binny directs remotely — **NOT launch-blocking**
- Order: anchor (Valencia/Metro/deed) → competence (Puzol delivered, SAP/Siemens) → story (⏳ verify biography per psych doc §3) → candor (not centro; no aval) → CTA ("tell me what still worries you") + freedom line.
- English-first (subtitled; DE/HE versions only if a segment proves out). Face, natural light, site or finished Puzol building behind him. No music under candor lines, no stock footage (P5: it's *his* face that converts).
- **Launch order:** A2 ships first with a natural photo of Uriel + the founder letter in text (same sequence) — that carries most of the identifiable-person effect (P14) on day one. Video lands as a week-2/3 upgrade; measure its lift when it arrives.
- **Prerequisite for letter *and* video: the biography verification call with Uriel (day 1). Nothing ships with unverified biography (P12).**

### A2 — Landing page (single screen-flow, English)
- Same sequence as A1 in text + embedded video. One number policy: floor/stress first, 7% only as labeled target (deal doc §3, P12).
- **Fear #0 — location legitimacy (F2) — is pre-answered in the page body, not offered as a button.** Brokers' first question is "why this unknown, not-near-the-sea town?" — end investors think it and don't say it. The page answers it before the CTA, in the fact chain: Metro L1 to Valencia · new+furnished rents ~40–55% above town average (17 live comps) · fastest-renting market in Spain, 24% of listings gone in <24h · near-zero vacancy (deal doc §5). Frame honestly per P7: "It's not a postcard town — it's where Valencia's workforce actually lives," *after* competence is established.
- CTA block: 4 fear buttons + free-text ("something else") → opens WhatsApp/email thread (offer both; Northern Europeans often prefer email — let the click data decide, log to field-data-log). The 4 fears are hypotheses, expect the free-text to teach us the real ones:
  1. "Rent will not come in" (B5) — the button version of fear #0 for those it still itches
  2. "Construction stops halfway" (B1/B5)
  3. "The 4% minimum is only on paper" (B3/B5)
  4. "Show me the deeds and licenses first" (B1)
  5. "Who actually answers, after I have paid?" (B5)
- Secondary, visually quiet CTA: "Prefer to just talk? Book 15 minutes with Uriel." — the primary path stays low-commitment (name a fear), but decisive buyers must never be forced through extra hoops. Log which path each lead takes (field-data-log).
- **Social proof policy: real only, ever.** Permitted: 1 of 10 units sold (deal doc §1); real broker/agency interest once listable (F5); and a growing **"Questions investors have asked so far"** section — real, dated, hard questions with honest answers, refreshed weekly from the fear log. It is simultaneously social proof (people engage), candor proof (look what he answers), and objection handling. Forbidden: any fabricated interest, viewer counters, or fake calendar density — one detected fake retroactively poisons every honest claim (P12, P9), and this audience is the best-trained in the world at detecting exactly that pattern.
- Compliance note: no "guaranteed return" phrasing anywhere in ad copy — both platform policy risk and P12 red line. The floor is described as what it is: a contractual covenant (deal doc §7).

### A3 — Fear-answer library (message-ready, one per fear) — Binny writes, Uriel fact-checks
- Each answer: acknowledge the fear as rational → the honest fact (with its ✅/🟡/⏳ status, from the deal doc) → one attachment max → one question back. Every claim traces to a deal-doc section.

### A4 — Live calls
- **Default: 1:1 call with Uriel**, booked after at least one fear-answer exchange (a call offered too early is a high-commitment ask — commitment gradient). Loose shape, not a script: hear their fear back → answer with paper on screen → agree the *next* concrete step (data room folder or counsel intro) → freedom line (P15).
- **Phase-2: group Q&A (conditional).** Trigger: ≥5–8 qualified fear-named leads available to invite. 25 minutes: Uriel live from Valencia (10 min story + building walk) → candor Q&A on the hardest collected questions (10 min) → next-step offer (5 min, freedom line). Unique payload: B6 peers-in-the-room + credible scarcity (P16). Never run with a thin room — empty seats are negative social proof.

### A6 — Search campaign (the data engine)
- Google Search, English, exact/phrase match on high-intent queries (buy apartment Valencia, new build Valencia investment, Spain rental property investment, etc. — validate volumes with free Keyword Planner first, log to field-data-log).
- Ad copy = fluent anchor + honesty marker, no yield in headlines (P4, P9). Budget cap €10–20/day. Primary KPI: **cost per named fear**.

### A5 — Weekly on-ground proof loop — Uriel, 2 min/week
- One raw 30–60s clip from the site/town per week (progress, the metro stop, the street). Distribution: WhatsApp status/group + landing page. Mechanism: repeated exposure builds familiarity (P3) and construction progress is the strongest running answer to fear #2.

## 5. Metrics + kill criteria (visible progress for a new relationship)

| Stage | Metric | Healthy signal (first 30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Search impressions/clicks · **cost per landing visit** | Data exists within 7 days of campaign start — this is the anchor dataset |
| Landing | Fear-CTA click rate · **cost per named fear** | ≥ several named fears per week |
| Dialogue | Reply after first answer | Conversations that continue = B3 flipping |
| Live | 1:1 calls with Uriel booked/held | First calls happening = the funnel's core transaction works |
| Evidence | Data-room requests | Paper-first buyers surfacing |
| Close | Reservations | 1 serious counsel conversation in month 1 = on track for a 9-unit pipeline |

**Kill/pivot rules:** if a channel produces zero named fears in 2 weeks, change the channel, not the message. If the *same* fear dominates free-text, the landing page reorders around it. Every number lands in `../field-data-log.md`; report the fear distribution to Uriel weekly — it's genuine market intelligence, not just marketing ops.

**Economic benchmark (F4 + `../engagement-terms.md`):** Uriel's broker channel pays 2–3% per sale ≈ **€4,600–7,000 per buyer**. The web funnel's own **€2,500/unit success fee is under half that**, and its fixed fees are offset against bonuses — so per buyer it is decisively the cheaper channel. Against a **€1,500 initial media cap**, even one web buyer over the run beats the broker channel on cost — the yardstick for every budget conversation.

## 7. Honest odds (so expectations survive contact)

Direct-to-cold-investor is the hardest funnel in this business — that was known when choosing it. What makes this version viable:

1. **Intent capture removes the hardest problem.** We never have to make a stranger care — we meet people already searching for exactly this, and only pay for those (A6).
2. **Nine sales is the whole job.** Ten right conversations outperform 100,000 impressions; a €15/day search budget is not a handicap at this scale.
3. **The funnel produces data before it produces sales** — search-term costs and the named-fear distribution land in the field log within week 1. "No data to anchor on" ends seven days after launch, by construction.
4. The failure mode to guard against is not silence but **overreach**: one 🟡 claim presented as ✅ to force momentum destroys the only real asset (P12). When in doubt, undersell the number and oversell the paper.
