# Founder hook video — script (70–90 seconds)

**Speaker:** Uriel Nabel (or on-camera director of IR if Uriel unavailable — still a human with authority, not stock voiceover)  
**Look:** Natural light, quiet room or building site / plot. Phone vertical or horizontal — consistency > polish. No luxury montage.  
**Tone:** Peer speaking to a careful allocator. Not a broker. Not a TED talk.

---

## On-camera script (teleprompter / prompt cards)

*(Speak slightly slower than normal. Pause after the honesty lines.)*

---

**[0:00–0:20 · Heart]**

Hi — I’m Uriel, founder of El Fortín.

I didn’t start this so I could sell “nine percent on a landing page.”

I started it because people I trust wanted a piece of Spain — real deeded brick — without becoming full-time landlords, and without betting their savings on a tourist license that can disappear with the next city ordinance.

---

**[0:20–0:45 · Honesty]**

So let me tell you what this is *not*.

It is not Valencia city centre. It’s Riba-roja — Metro Line 1, airport close, suburban, real. If you want a trophy address in Eixample, this isn’t your deal, and I won’t pretend it is.

It is also not a bank guarantee. The income floor we write into the management agreement is a **developer covenant** — backed by our skin in the game: land already paid, our margin tied to finishing and operating this building — not by a Spanish bank standing behind us forever.

If that already feels too soft for you: good. You’re the kind of person who should keep their cash until the documents convince you.

---

**[0:45–1:05 · Stakes]**

What we *are* offering is a small ten-unit building we control from the ground up — titles in your name, flexible rental rights designed into the structure of the asset, professional operations, and numbers that hold up even when we stress the boring case: everything going long-term.

I’m not asking you to believe a brochure. I’m asking you to put me under the same questions you’d ask if we were sitting across a table.

---

**[1:05–1:25 · Door / micro-loop]**

Here’s the only ask:  
reply — and tell me **which fear is loudest** right now.

Is it rent if demand collapses…  
construction if we stall mid-build…  
whether the “four percent floor” is real…  
or do you just want the deed and license paper first.

I’ll send back **one thing** that answers *that* — not a dump of PDFs.

If after that you still want to talk, we’ll talk. If not — you were right to walk. That’s fine with me.

---

**[END · optional lower-third / text on screen]**

El Fortín Capital · Riba-roja, Valencia metro  
Reply with your #1 concern

---

## Shot list (keep it human)

| Time | Visual |
|---|---|
| Opening | Face, still. Name + “El Fortín” once on screen |
| Honesty block | Optional cutaway: metro sign / street / site — 2–3 seconds max, not a reel |
| Stakes | Back to face |
| CTA | Face + text: “What’s your #1 fear?” |

**Audio:** Room tone OK. No music bed under the honesty lines. Soft ambient only if it doesn’t sound “proptech.”

---

## Post caption / email embed line

> 70 seconds of honesty before any numbers: what El Fortín is — and isn’t. Reply with your #1 concern and I’ll send *one* document, not a brochure.

---

## Length discipline

If the cut runs over 90 seconds, **cut lifestyle language first**, then cut project adjectives. Never cut the lines about Riba-roja / not a bank aval / choose your fear.
