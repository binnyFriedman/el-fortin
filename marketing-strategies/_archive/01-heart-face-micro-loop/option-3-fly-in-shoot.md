# Option 3 — Fly-in shoot (your gear)

**Model:** You fly to Valencia with your camera/audio kit. You direct and shoot.  
**They cover:** flight + accommodation.  
**Shooting day itself:** covered (no day rate from them — that is the deal).  
**Deliverable:** Strategy 01 founder hook (70–90s) + usable selects for fear-branch clips / stills.

Compare:

| | Option 1 — Local DP | Option 2 — Uriel iPhone | **Option 3 — You fly in** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost to them | DP day rate + expenses | Tripod + lav only | Flight + hotel |
| Quality control | Variable | Risky (sound) | **You** |
| Uriel’s job | Talent only | Talent + operator | **Talent only** |
| Doc for talent | Soft notes | [`iphone-diy-shoot-guide.md`](iphone-diy-shoot-guide.md) | Show-up time + wardrobe + script only |
| Shot bible for you | [`production-shot-list.md`](production-shot-list.md) | — | This page + production-shot-list |

## Commercial terms (keep explicit in the email)

**Included (them)**

- Return flight [city] → Valencia (VLC) — economy, dates agreed  
- Accommodation — X nights near Riba-roja / Valencia (quiet hotel; early call)  
- Local transport on shoot day (car / Uriel drives site ↔ metro ↔ Puzol)  
- Meals on shoot day (simple)

**Included (you)**

- Camera + audio kit you already own  
- Direction, shooting, logging  
- One hero cut (70–90s) + 9:16 crop + selects folder (timeline agreed, e.g. 7–10 days after)

**Not included unless renegotiated**

- Your day rate / editorial fee beyond the agreed cut  
- Stock footage licences (if needed — bill separately or skip)  
- Second shoot day / major pickup travel  
- Colour grade beyond a clean honest pass  
- Music licensing

**One-line offer you can send**

> I fly with my gear. You cover flight + hotel. Shoot day is on me. You get a directed founder film with proper sound — Uriel only shows up and speaks.

## Why Option 3 wins for this strategy

Strategy 01 lives or dies on **face + trust + clean honesty lines**.  
Option 2 is cheap but audio often fails.  
Option 1 depends on a stranger reading the room.  
**Option 3:** you already know the psychology of the cut — you shoot for the fear CTA ending, not for a pretty brochure.

Flight + hotel is the fee for that control.
