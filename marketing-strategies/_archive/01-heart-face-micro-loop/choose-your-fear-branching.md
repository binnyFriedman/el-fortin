# Choose-your-fear — branching replies

Use after the founder video or as buttons on a landing page / WhatsApp list.

**Rule:** One fear → one focused answer → one next step. No full data-room dump until they ask or name counsel.

## Fear 1 — Rent collapses (Riba-roja / industrial / €540)

**Subject / opener:** You asked about the rent floor. Fair.

**Body (copy):**

```
You’re right that a local unfurnished family rent on a small Riba-roja flat isn’t €1,300.

At roughly €11–12/m², a ~47 m² unit looks more like ~€540/month unfurnished. That’s the floor of the *local* market — and we underwrite knowing that.

What we’re actually operating toward is a *mix*: mid-term furnished and flex use where the building allows it — with a stress case where the whole building goes long-term furnished. In our sheet, that stress portfolio still nets around ~4.3% on all-in capital. The contractual 4% floor exists for the gap when reality is uglier than the model.

I’m attaching the dual-scenario one-pager (target mix vs all long-term). If you want the next layer, tell me and I’ll send only the yield tab from the underwriting file — not a marketing pack.
```

**Attach:** `b2b/investment-teaser-one-pager.html` → PDF section II.

## Fear 2 — Construction stall (phased capital trap)

**Subject / opener:** Mid-build risk — you nailed the hard part.

**Body (copy):**

```
Phased payments don’t make your money safe if we stall at 50%. You were right about that. Once capital is in the walls without an LPO, you’re in recovery / completion territory — the yield guarantee doesn’t run during construction.

What we’re putting on the table instead of slogans:
• License and land status in Folder 02
• Milestone payments against certified progress
• Clear cure / delay / rescission language in the purchase track
• Investor remedies if handover slips past a long-stop (your lawyer will redline this)

I’ll send the reservation / milestones skeleton and the open questions we still need to harden (performance bond, retención, long-stop). If those documents look thin when your counsel reads them, you should walk. I’d rather lose you on paper than trap you in a shell.
```

**Attach / promise:** Folder `03` milestone draft when ready; until then be honest that it’s pending.

## Fear 3 — Guarantor / 4% floor is a mirage

**Subject / opener:** Who guarantees the guarantor.

**Body (copy):**

```
Straight answer: there is no aval bancario on the income floor today. It is a contractual covenant of the SL — as strong as our solvency and your written remedies.

What backs the *story* is not magic money: land paid, ~€299k project profit at sell-out tied to finishing and operating, and — most important for you — step-in / termination language when we miss the floor or stall. A guarantee without removal rights is theatre.

I’m sending (or prioritizing) Management Agreement Section 4: default, cure, and investor step-in / collective removal. Read that before you read any yield chart. If Section 4 is weak, make us strengthen it or keep the cash in Frankfurt.
```

**Attach:** Data room Folder 03 — step-in PDF when live. Until live: don’t overclaim; invite counsel redlines.
