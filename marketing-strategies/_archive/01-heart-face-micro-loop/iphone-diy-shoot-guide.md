# iPhone DIY shoot guide — Uriel

**This is your whole brief.** Shoot only what’s on this page. Someone else handles the rest of the edit.

**Gear (buy or rent):** iPhone that can do 4K · **tripod** with phone clamp · **lav / body mic** into the phone (Lightning or USB-C) · headphones to check sound · optional cheap reflector.

**Script:** [`founder-video-script.md`](founder-video-script.md)

---

## 1. What “good enough” means

| Win | Don’t chase |
|---|---|
| Clear face, clear voice, honest eyes | Cinema look |
| One clean ~90s talking take | Perfect acting |
| A few real clips of the Riba-roja site | Every location in Valencia |

If the **audio is clean** and you look like someone people would trust across a table — you’re done.

---

## 2. Setup before you leave home

| Item | What to get |
|---|---|
| Tripod | Phone mount · tall enough for **eye-level** standing shot |
| Lav mic | Clip-on into Lightning or USB-C (Rode / similar budget — not the €8 no-name) |
| Adapter | If mic is 3.5mm, use a dongle that **actually supports a microphone** |

**Phone settings**

1. Clean the lens.  
2. **Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 25 fps** (or 4K 30 — pick one and keep it all day).  
3. **Off:** Cinematic mode.  
4. **Do Not Disturb** on. Airplane mode if you can (calls kill takes).  
5. Free **~20 GB**. Charge to **100%** + power bank.

---

## 3. Sound first

1. Clip lav on shirt/jacket, ~15–20 cm under your chin. Hide the cable.  
2. Plug mic in **before** opening Camera.  
3. Record a **10-second test** of your first script line. Play back **with headphones**.  
4. Prefer **shade + little wind**. Turn your back to wind; use collar / foam windshield if you have one.  
5. Stay away from generators, AC fans, and busy roadside if you can.

**Simplest method:** Camera app + lav → video and sound together.

**If that sounds bad:** Use a second phone (or Voice Memos) for audio with the lav, clap once in front of the camera so the takes can be synced later. Only if you must.

**Rule:** After every take, listen to the first few seconds. If you wouldn’t trust it on a Zoom call — do it again.

---

## 4. Camera (so it doesn’t look like a selfie)

1. Tripod at **your eye height**. Phone **horizontal** (landscape).  
2. Frame: **chest to top of head**, a little space above your hair.  
3. Eye level — not up your nose, not looking down.  
4. Face **open shade** (side of a building). No harsh noon sun splitting your face.  
5. Background: the site (fence / structure), a couple of metres behind you if you can. Clear trash from frame.  
6. Wipe the lens again.  
7. Tap your face → hold until **AE/AF Lock** → then hit Record.

---

## 5. What to shoot — checklist

### Talking piece (the main film)

Put the script on a second phone next to the lens (out of frame). On the last “reply with your fear” line, look **into** the lens.

```
[ ] Tripod · AE/AF Lock · lav tested in headphones
[ ] Take 1 — full script
[ ] Take 2 — full script (often the best)
[ ] Take 3 — full script (safety)
[ ] Short pickups (each its own clip):
      [ ] “It’s Riba-roja — Metro Line 1, airport close, suburban, real.”
      [ ] “It is also not a bank guarantee.”
      [ ] “Reply and tell me which fear is loudest right now.”
      [ ] “Uriel Nabel, founder of El Fortín.”
[ ] Listen to each take before you leave the spot
```

**How to speak**

- A bit **slower** than normal.  
- Pause after the honest lines.  
- If you blank: stop, breathe, restart from the start of that paragraph.  
- A real take with a small stumble beats a perfect dead take.

### Place clips (handheld OK — no talking)

Two hands on the phone, elbows in. Each clip **4–6 seconds**. Silent.

```
[ ] B1  Street / address — Camino or Carretera Valencia · Riba-roja
[ ] B2  Wide of the plot / building / fencing
[ ] B3  Your hands on plans or a tablet on site
[ ] B4  You walking onto the site (friend films, or timer / propped phone)
[ ] B5  Close detail — concrete, brick, tools (~3 seconds)
[ ] B6  Ordinary street near the site (~3 seconds)
```

### Optional (only if you still have energy)

```
[ ] Metro station exterior near Riba-roja (signage)
[ ] Puzol: exterior of the finished building + you at the door (2 clips)
[ ] 10 still photos of your face in the same light (Portrait mode OK for photos)
```

**Don’t:** selfie walk-and-talk as the only version · vertical-only talking piece · heavy filters · drone near the airport.

---

## 6. Day plan

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Night before | Charge · print / save script · clear storage · set 4K |
| Morning at site | Soft light → mic test → talking takes → listen → B1–B6 |
| If wind ruined sound | Redo talking piece indoors by a quiet window (same framing) |
| Optional later | Puzol (2 clips) |

**You’re done when you have:** 2 good full talking takes + B1 + B2 + B3.

---

## 7. Send the files

1. Folder name: `ElFortin_Hook_YYYYMMDD`.  
2. Label roughly: `A_take01` · `A_take02` · `B1_address` …  
3. **Do not** send via WhatsApp (it crushes quality).  
4. AirDrop / Google Drive / WeTransfer — **original files**.  
5. Short note: which talking take is best, e.g. “Take 2 is the one. Audio checked OK.”

---

## 8. If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wind noise | Behind a wall/car · back to wind · collar over lav · move indoors |
| Face too dark | Face open sky (not the sun) · shade · reflector under chin |
| Phone hot | Shade it · shorter takes · cool down 5 min |
| Focus jumping | AE/AF Lock on your face before Record |
| Feeling “salesy” | Softer face · smaller smile · talk to one smart friend behind the phone |
| Only filmed vertical | Re-do the talking piece in landscape |

---
