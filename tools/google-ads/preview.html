How the new ad can appear

One Dutch responsive search ad. Google shows at most three headlines and two descriptions, and it chooses the mix. The AFM sentence is pinned as the first description, so it is on every impression. A search ad cannot carry the AFM graphic. The right-hand column is the English of that same impression. The click goes to the Dutch page. The words after the domain are display text, not the address.

The lines Google can use

Fifteen headlines and four descriptions, which is the maximum. Headlines stop at 30 characters, descriptions at 90, paths at 15. The first description is pinned. Google will not usually pair the two August lines. “Indicatief, geen aanbod” is a headline only, so it appears only when Google picks it.
